# Open questions for the client

Never assume an answer. When a question is answered, write the answer and the date, then update any
provisional decision in `DECISIONS.md` that depends on it.

Use `prototype/index.html` during the workshop: clients answer more precisely when they point at a screen.

Answers dated 2026-10-09 and 2026-10-10 were given by Mahmoud in a working session. Items marked
**To confirm** are details still missing; ask them at the client workshop (P0-T01).

| Q1 | **Profit basis.** Does each partner supplier get a % of the net profit of **their own products**, a % of **total company** profit, or does it differ per supplier? | Phase 6 | **Own products only** (2026-10-09). Each supplier gets a % of the net profit made on the products they supplied. |
| Q2 | **Expense allocation.** How are shared costs (fuel, salaries, electricity…) split between suppliers: by sales, by gross profit, by nature of the cost, or decided by the owner each month? Is it written in the contracts? | P6-T04 | **By nature of the cost** (2026-10-09, detailed 2026-10-10). Storage costs (electricity, cold room) by kg held in stock; fuel, salaries and administration by sales. The rules are a verbal agreement with the suppliers, not written in the contracts. **Updated 2026-10-11 (client meeting, D-041):** costs are charged **per lot**: typed costs, day labour, monthly employees at days × salary / 26, cold room by a weight per room then by kg held per day; rent and the accountant are included (split by sales, provisional). |
| Q3 | **Investor capital.** When a supplier invests money, is the capital paid back separately from the profit share? When? What happens in a month with a loss? | P6-T02, P6-T07 | **Repaid through the profit share** (2026-10-09): monthly payments first repay the capital, then count as profit once it is fully repaid. **Loss month: the supplier carries their % of the loss, deducted from their capital** (2026-10-10). **Two rates per partner** (2026-10-10): one % applied in a profit month, another % in a loss month; they may differ. **Provisional (D-031):** the part of a loss share that the remaining capital cannot cover, or the whole loss share of a partner without capital, is carried as an amount the partner owes and is recovered from their next profit shares. **To confirm with the client.** **Updated 2026-10-11 (client meeting, D-040):** the partner is a supplier who puts money into **one lot** with the owner; profit and loss are shared **per lot**, the loss share still deducted from their capital. |
| Q4 | **Same product, several suppliers.** Can Crevette Royale come from two suppliers at different prices in the same month? | D-009, P1-T05 | **Yes, tracked separately** (2026-10-09). Each supplier's batches stay separate so profit goes to the right supplier. |
| Q5 | **Invoices and tax.** VAT rates on each product? Fiscal stamp amount? Is the business required to use TTN / El Fatoora e-invoicing? Withholding tax (retenue à la source) with some customers? | P2-T05 | (2026-10-09, detailed 2026-10-10) **VAT:** one rate for all products, 19 %. **Prices:** whether sale prices include VAT is a company setting. **Fiscal stamp:** 1.000 DT per invoice. **Withholding tax:** yes with some customers; optional per invoice, chosen before printing; the rate is editable in the finance settings and when it is 0 % nothing is shown on the invoice; tracked in the finance module. **TTN / El Fatoora:** required now, but **not implemented for now** (2026-10-10, D-016). **To confirm with the accountant:** the VAT code lists Tunisian fishing products as exempt, which does not match 19 % for all; and e-invoicing has been mandatory for VAT-registered businesses since 01/01/2026 (see `docs/spec/sample/README.md`). |
| Q6 | **Payroll.** How many employees? Monthly fixed salary, daily pay, or commission? Which employer contributions (CNSS) must be tracked? | P5-T03 | **Fixed monthly salary; employer CNSS contributions are tracked** as a monthly expense; **6 to 10 employees** (2026-10-10). |
| Q7 | **Costing.** Is a sold kilo valued at its own batch cost (FIFO) or at an average cost? Purchase prices change from one day to the next (2026-10-10), so each receipt sets the cost of its own batch. **Added 2026-10-11:** the owner also wants the weighted **average purchase price** per product, for pricing only (D-040). |
| Q8 | **Locations.** How many cold rooms / freezers and trucks? The exact truck count comes with the client's truck list. |
| Q9 | **Month-end.** Who approves the closing? Is profit calculated on sales made (accrual) or on cash received? | P6-T06 | **Profit on sales made (accrual). | Q25 | **General costs: days counted.** Rent, accountant and the like are charged monthly amount / 26 for **each calendar day** with stock (D-041, D-059), so a 30-day month adds up to 30/26 = 115 % of the cost and a 31-day month to 31/26 = 119 % when stock is held every day: lots together carry more than the bill. Which is meant: a 26th for each *working* day only (so a month adds up to at most 100 %), or the cost spread over every calendar day? | P5-T05, P6 | _recommended default, not applied (formula unchanged):_ **calendar days: monthly amount / days in the month**, so a month always adds up to exactly 100 % of the bill. Salaries (days on a lot × salary / 26, D-041) are not affected. Follows Q21. |
| Q26 | **Employee CNSS.** What rate is withheld from the employee's gross salary for their own CNSS contribution, and is anything else withheld by law (income tax, the solidarity contribution)? The employer's 17,07 % is already a provisional default (D-055). | P5-T04 | _provisional (2026-10-12, D-119):_ **9,18 %** of the gross, shown on its own line of the payslip figures and subtracted from the net, per employee and changeable per month. Income tax (IRPP) and other legal withholdings are **not** computed: they stay in "deductions" typed by hand. **The accountant confirms.** |
| Q27 | **Returnable boxes (caisses).** Two sizes (small, big). Whose are they, can they be borrowed from suppliers, deposits, lost boxes, lot tracking, weights? | P1-T14, P3 | **Answered 2026-10-13:** boxes belong to the company; they can also be received from a supplier and **must be returned** to that supplier. **No deposits** (no money in the box ledger). **Lost or broken boxes are company overhead**: not charged to clients or drivers, and not part of a lot's costs. **No lot tracking**: boxes are counted per location and per client. **No tare**: box weight is not in the kg figures. A box left at a client is counted as out of the truck until it is collected. |
| Q28 | **Buying at the port (achat terrain).** Who buys, how is the supplier paid, who checks? | P3-T12 | **Answered 2026-10-15 (owner, D-176):** some drivers (not all) buy at the port or from fishermen: supplier (from the list, or a new name), products (from the list, or a new name), kg, price per kg, money given, rest to pay. They send it as a draft and can still change it until it is validated; every change after sending is kept as a revision. The **owner or the manager** checks and validates it; they fix what is wrong themselves and leave a note to the driver. Only then do the stock, the debt and the payment count. **We owe the kg really received** (the driver and the fisherman can both be wrong). The deal is set **per purchase** (one supplier can be paid at once, at N days or monthly). The driver can pay **cash or by cheque** (post-dated cheques and traites exist); a paper invoice from the supplier is optional. The office gives the driver a sum for purchases in the morning; he may buy more than that (the rest is owed). He does **not** see what the company already owed the supplier. The responsable chooses where the goods go. A purchase may be for a partner lot. A purchase not validated in time raises an alert. The driver may add a new supplier from the phone. _Provisional: the alert after 24 hours._ |
